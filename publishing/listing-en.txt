Name: Basair
Short description: An offline Arabic Islamic library with sources and four-school comparisons.

Basair is an Arabic Islamic reading library with original book pages and transparent source and reuse information.

Explore seven subjects: Islamic jurisprudence, creed, hadith, prophetic biography, Quran commentary, biographies of early Muslims, and spiritual purification.

This version contains 12 books and volumes representing 11 titles, with 2,410 original PDF pages available offline. Contents include Al-Fiqh Al-Muyassar, the Tahawi creed, the Nawawi hadith collection with Ibn Rajab's additions, Tafsir Al-Jalalayn with its included margins, biographies of the four imams, volumes 1 and 2 of Sifat Al-Safwa, Al-Sa'di's means to a happy life, and Ibn Al-Qayyim's letter to a brother.

Reading features:
• View and zoom original book pages and jump to a page.
• Save books and resume your reading position.
• Search Arabic book titles, authors and topics.
• Browse shortcuts for the four imams, companions, successors and purification topics.
• Read 15 concise comparisons across the four Sunni legal schools, with source, volume and page references. Search comparisons and filter by topic and school.
• Inspect the author, edition, source link and reuse conditions for each included file.

The books and reading interface are in Arabic. Search covers metadata and comparison summaries, not every word in scanned pages. The two included Sifat Al-Safwa volumes are not the complete multi-volume work. External source links require internet access.

No account or advertisements. Reading preferences are stored on your device. Comparisons are educational summaries, not personal fatwas.
